# Principles

## One goal and three axes

- Goal = improve human–agent collaboration efficiency.
- Skill = procedures and checks; curb unsupported assumptions and unnecessary edits.
- Persona = consistent conversation and situational responses; support collaboration experience.
- Flow = scope, state, verification and handoff; Role = stage-specific expertise.
- Efficiency and token savings = goals requiring product-specific comparative measurement.

## Authority and boundaries

- Keep rules in one authoritative source; deliver pointers to clients.
- Execute against the user request, design and change boundaries.
- Route generated-file changes through their sources and generators.
- Manage ticket state and indexes through CLI; separate plans from completion evidence.
- Preserve previous work, failure evidence and user changes.

## Verification and expression

- Completion = actual results, verification evidence and remaining risks together.
- Missing, unexecuted and unverified results remain explicit.
- Persona governs expression; technical facts and quality judgments retain evidence.
- Research figures belong to their experiments; separate from product-specific performance.
